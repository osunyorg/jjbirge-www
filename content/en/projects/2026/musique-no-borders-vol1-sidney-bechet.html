Lionel Martin - soprano & tenor sax
Jean-Jacques Birgé - piano, field recording & machines

The first release in the Musique No Borders collection, this Volume 1 is a tribute to Sidney Bechet (external link), the main source of inspiration for saxophonist Lionel Martin, also known as Madsaxx. For this debut album, he has enlisted the help of Jean-Jacques Birgé, a master of electronic and improvised sound. An album that transcends artistic boundaries, bringing together painting and music thanks to the collaboration with painter Robert Combas.

Recorded and mixed at Studio GRRR (Paris) and SDB Studio (Vernaison) by the musicians in 2025. Master by Cédric Beron a.k.a. Disque Noir

Out on October 16 2026

Compositions - Sidney Bechet (Petite Fleur, Les Oignons)
Arrangements - Lionel Martin & Jean-Jacques Birgé
Mastering - Cedric Beron a.k.a. Disque Noir
Painting* - Robert Combas
* "Le paysage plein d'arbustes à fleurs plus champignons pignoleurs en bas de page. Une véritable ménagerie d'oiseaux se cachent et foutent le boxon dans ces feuillages délicats fleurissars et ornementalo." 1985, acrylic on canvas, 208*206 cm.

45 rpm 7"
