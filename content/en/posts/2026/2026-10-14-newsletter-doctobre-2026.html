Release of the Sidney Bechet 45 rpm single featuring Lionel Martin, soundtrack to Carol Müller’s film Nachtwache (am Teufelsberg), and recent LP release Rock ’n Roll Station Redux Extraction by Nurse With Wound. I also mention that I’ve started reviewing records for the excellent online magazine Citizen Jazz, but I don’t say anything about my two new instruments—the Enigma synthesizer and an electric đàn bầu I brought back from Vietnam, which I’m learning to master. I’m never quite satisfied with making myself useful. “Doing the dishes”—these days, that means, for example, lugging around the entire inventory of the GRRR record collection, which involves lifting boxes of LP records that weigh a ton or crawling to the back of the attic to count the CDs. Of course, I have big plans that I’ll reveal in due time, and I always leave room for the unexpected—the kind of thing that makes my heart race when it happens.

Single SIDNEY BECHET by Lionel MARTIN & Jean-Jacques BIRGÉ































 Les oignons / Petite fleur. Beautiful cover by ROBERT COMBAS. Only 300 exemplaries - 16€. First volume of a new collection of label Ouch ! Distribution InOuïe Order it on Bandcamp ... SIDNEY BECHET is one of LIONEL MARTIN’s heroes. I imagine he thought of me because I had played on his lap and blown into his soprano saxophone—my earliest memory of music. I was five years old. I play the piano and various electronic devices, to which I’ve added some little birds I caught in Tangier in 2003 and a boxing match that Sidney attended after faking an illness—a move that canceled a performance of the operetta New Orleans , which would ruin my father, who had produced it in 1958. Lionel plays soprano and tenor. I should even write “tenors,” since there are so many of them on Les oignons . Should I mention that I use random feedback on  Petite Fleur  ?


Music for Carol Müller's film
NACHTWACHE AM TEUFELSBERG































  A frantic race through the night on the Teufelsberg. Flames of light tear through the darkness, the forest opens up, bodies struggle, the landscape falls into a trance. In the distance: bomber planes, stray bullets, crumbling ruins, the sounds emanating from Cold War military installations, and animals panting. Between forest and ruins, Teufelsberg is a landscape holding its breath, laden with layers of war, surveillance, and memory. The film is a nocturnal vigil where the past surfaces less as an image than as a murmur.
Film directed by  Carol MÜLLER .
Hörspiel (musical score) by Jean-Jacques BIRGÉ. 3 minutes. Soon on Vimeo...


NURSE WITH WOUND - LP

ROCK 'N ROLL STATION REDUX EXTRACTION
































Rock 'n Roll Station (Redux Extraction)  
LP 30cm / 4 remixes de NURSE WITH WOUND  by Jean-Jacques BIRGÉ, IRR. APP. (EXT.) ,  Diarmuid MacDIARMADA  and  Andrew LILES . A sneak peek at the 5-CD box set dedicated to the greatly expanded reissue of the album Rock 'n Roll Station . Originally inspired by Jac Berrocal’s cult track featuring Vince Taylor, released in 1977 on his LP Parallèles , the album by Nurse With Wound—Steven Stapleton’s band, known among other things for the famous list, the underground bible that features Birgé Gorgé Shiroc’s Défense de , is significantly expanded thanks to numerous remixes of the seven tracks from the NWW album. The box set will be released soon on the British label Dirter, and will include my own remix of  A Silhouette And A Thumbtack (Dance in Hyperspace) .


I'm back at the must-read online magazine Citizen Jazz . I’m very happy to be joining the editorial team to write columns that will also appear on my blog.
