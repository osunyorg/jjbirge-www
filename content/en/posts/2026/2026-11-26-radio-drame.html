Radio Drame (external link) has been fixed. You can once again listen to 206 hours of nonstop music, without interruption. The 1,729 tracks are played in random order, and the deck is reshuffled every time you load the homepage (external link). You can, however, go back or skip to the next track.

If you prefer to listen to the albums as they were originally conceived, simply choose one of the 176 albums, organized into two categories: online albums (external link) (only on Internet) and CD/LP (external link). The format is MP3, and the quality is generally good. However, sound quality depends primarily on your audio system, amplifier and speakers.

84 of them are also available on Bandcamp (external link), where you can listen to them, download them, and, most importantly, order them, either digitally (WAV 44.16 format) or as CDs in their original physical packaging, which we've always taken great care to design.

For LPs, the easiest way is to go through a record store, while they still exist, such as Souffle Continu (external link) or Dizonord (external link) in Paris, Les Allumés du Jazz (external link) in Le Mans, etc., or to visit us at Studio GRRR, the label's headquarters, founded in 1975.

P.S.: Thanks to Sacha André (external link), who created the new website and blog and is taking care of the final touches as we go.
