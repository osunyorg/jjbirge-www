DÉLIVRÉS DES LIVRES (external link)
Instantaneous compositions recorded at Studio GRRR by Jean-Jacques Birgé on November 4, 2026 in the order they were played. Inspired by 11 pages of Le livre des livres (external link) by Marc-Antoine Mathieu (external link) (Éditions Delcourt) and mixed on November 5 by JJB. With François Jeanneau on soprano sax, flute and for the first time on Aerophone AE30, Paolo Rezze on electric cello and Jean-Jacques Birgé on keyboards, synthesizers, etc.
